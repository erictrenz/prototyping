Frequently asked questions

Will checking my options hurt my credit score?

Many lenders offer pre-qualification with a soft credit inquiry. Hard inquiries typically happen later in the application process.

How fast can I get the money?

Timing varies by lender and verification. Some approvals can fund quickly, while complex applications may take longer.

What documents do I need?

Common requirements include identity, income verification, and banking details. Specific requirements vary by lender.

Can I use a loan to pay bills?

Often yes, but restrictions can apply. Review lender terms and legal disclosures before accepting a loan.
